Welcome to XP-Deck - First-Time Setup

XP-Deck helps you catalogue every game you have ever played.

It shows you one game at a time and you sort it with a swipe or a key: Played Backlog Skipped. Everything stays on this computer, and you can export it to a spreadsheet or Playnite whenever you like.

Step 1 — Get your free game-database keys

Game covers and descriptions come from IGDB, which is owned by Twitch, so Twitch hands out the keys. It is free, takes about two minutes, and does not need a credit card.

  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps/create and sign in (any Twitch account works).
  2. Name: anything, for example My XP-Deck.
  3. OAuth Redirect URL: type http://localhost.
  4. Category: choose Application Integration, then Create.
  5. On the app you just made, click Manage, then New Secret.
  6. Copy the Client ID and the Client Secret into the boxes below.
Step 2 — Paste them here

These are saved to a file called .env next to the app and are never sent anywhere except Twitch.
